## #246, the design review of that-tooltip and that-button

**The morning session was flawed, and said so.** Its screen showed only the end-anchored placement
and the button padding; the tooltip's skin was applied from a 2026-09-29 comment the owner did not
recall making as a decision. Worse, the mock rows used the class `card`, and the companion frame's own
`.card { overflow: hidden }` clipped every tooltip on the screen. **Lesson:** a companion mock must not
use the frame's class names (`card`, `cards`, `mockup`, `option`, `options` and the rest in
`frame-template.html`), and a design session shows every difference in the ticket, never one carried in
from a comment. The companion also sends `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, so a screen cannot iframe another.

**The afternoon session, in the full prototype, settled:** the drawing's text and box (12px / 500,
5px 9px, no shadow), 2px corners over 4px, a **new 4px arrow** in every direction (at 2px with no arrow
it was not always clear what a tooltip belonged to), a **new ground `oklch(0.34 0.01 60)`** (0.06
vanished on the rail, 0.26 on the dark card), the flush end placement, and 12px button padding.
Recorded on #246, in the prototype and in the visual language spec. PR #266 carries it, after two
whole-branch reviews (fixed: inherited tracking and case; a long word overflowing a wrapping tooltip
once `overflow-visible` lifted the UA's clipping).

**Accessibility check of the ground:** text 10.55:1 (AAA). Box against dark grounds 1.45–1.74:1;
WCAG does not require 3:1 there. It turned up **#267**: a tooltip cannot be dismissed with Escape or
hovered (WCAG 1.4.13). Filed at intake; **approved by the owner** to `status/needs-refinement`, v1. It
conflicts with #107's `pointer-events: none`, so the two need one answer.
