The morning opened against a board that had moved without this machine knowing. `git fetch` was

**One hygiene defect, fixed by hand.** #11 closed carrying `status/in-progress`: the forge closes an
issue through `Closes #<n>` but does not touch its labels. Removed, and the rule promoted into
`handoff.md` — check the status label after every merge, because the merge will not do it.

**The branch ledger was re-verified, and `git branch --merged` was found to under-report it.** The
repository merges by rebase, so a merged branch is not an ancestor of `main` and that command misses
it. Four branches were being carried as unmerged for that reason alone. `git cherry codeberg/main
<branch>` is the check that tells the truth; seventeen local branches are droppable and only
`worktrees-without-the-tax` is genuinely unmerged.

issues — #8 and #10 ready, 24 in refinement, #74 at intake.
