Why

It is the only identity the app already has. The rail says WORK DASHBOARD in magenta on the dark ground, and this is that, at icon size — nothing invented.

Cost

Two letters is the most crowded a 32px icon can get, and WD belongs to a hundred other apps. Shipping it means drawing the letterforms as outlines, since a webfont cannot be relied on inside an icon file.
