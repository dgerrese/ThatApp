# CB#117: The shipped UI has drifted from the prototype, and the count is unknown

Archived from Codeberg issue [#117](https://codeberg.org/DionGerrese/ThatApp/issues/117), closed. Opened by DionGerrese on 2026-09-08, closed on 2026-09-09.

Labels: `bug`, `priority/high`.

Milestone: v1.

**Why it is kept:** Cited by CLAUDE.md/ticket-workflow ('why [CB#117](117.md) exists'); holds the 57-difference audit and owner rulings, incl. declined Push-becomes-Retry and stepper-at-0:30, recorded nowhere else.

## Body

#### Problem

The shipped UI has drifted from the design that was drawn for it, and nothing catches it. Two gaps
were found on 2026-09-08 by reading the prototype while designing CB#10's fetch control — neither was
found by a test, a review or a gate.

The prototype of record is `docs/superpowers/prototypes/2026-09-03-app-prototype.html`, and the
visual language it realises is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-03-visual-language-design.md`.

**The audit ran on 2026-09-08, view by view.** It found **57 differences**: twelve in the rail and
shell, fourteen in Today, fourteen in Week, ten in the entries dialog and seven in Settings. Most
are a class string or a padding value. A handful are larger. Every one was then checked against all
41 open issues, and the answer to "is this already somebody's job" was **no for fifty of them**.

**Two declared design tokens with no consumer anywhere are the fingerprint of this drift.**
`--color-primary-rail-ink` and `--color-pending-rail` exist in `web/src/styles.css` and are used by
nothing, because the two controls meant to wear them never got the class. So the Push button never
turns magenta when it is live, and the unpushed count never turns amber. A rail with three entries
waiting looks tonally identical to one with nothing to push, which is the exact "am I done" signal
the first edition built that colour for.

#### Approach

**This ticket fixes everything no existing issue already covers.** Settings is the one region
handed away whole — all seven of its findings are covered by **[CB#89](89.md)**, quoting [CB#89](89.md)'s own body on the
cog and modal, the form becoming an action, icon buttons on rows, health at rest with a timestamp,
the form's grid and the three-way segment. Two sub-parts of that region ride on [CB#89](89.md) without being
named in it, and are added to [CB#89](89.md) rather than built here: **the instance dot and host line on each
row**, and **whether a connection-test timestamp survives a reload**, which needs stored data [CB#89](89.md)
never asks for.

Eighteen findings sit beside an issue that would not have fixed them — CB#91 moves Week's rendering
functions while ruling out any rendering change, [CB#83](83.md) rebuilds Today's headers while asking nothing
about their type treatment, CB#108 recolours focus rings on controls that have one rather than adding
one where there is none. **They are fixed here.** That has a sequencing consequence: **[CB#117](117.md) lands
before CB#91 and [CB#83](83.md)**, because all three touch the same functions and [CB#117](117.md) is the one changing what
they render.

**Where the app is right and the drawing is stale, the prototype is corrected**, not annotated and
not left. The next ticket reads the drawing, so a drawing with a list of exceptions attached is the
failure this ticket exists to stop.

**Two disputed points were settled by the owner on 2026-09-08.** The rail becomes fixed beside a
scrolling viewport, and the divider below Push is restored at its drawn thickness — which overrides
the first edition's "nav sits directly under Push with no divider", so that line is corrected too.
**Two were declined:** Push does not become Retry after a failed push, and the entry dialog's
stepper keeps opening at `0:00` rather than the prototype's `0:30`.

**A language decision came out of W5 on 2026-09-09**, and is recorded as **Decision 12** in
[`2026-09-03-visual-language-design.md`](../../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-03-visual-language-design.md):
a toolbar button carries words, an inline control carries a glyph alone. Two exemptions were settled
with it — a paired directional group such as Week's arrows is one control with the words in its
middle button, and a window control such as a dialog's close is chrome rather than an action on the
view's data. It reaches beyond Week: Today's refresh gains a label, which is **T15**.

#### Acceptance criteria

##### Rail and app shell

- [x] **R1** The live Push button wears the primary fill and its ink, with the drawn hover
      brightening — `web/index.html:99`, consuming `--color-primary-rail-ink`. The disabled state is
      already right and does not change
- [x] **R2** The unpushed count wears the pending amber when there is anything to push, consuming
      `--color-pending-rail` — `paintUnpushed`, `web/src/rail.ts:104-111`
- [x] **R3** Push reads "Push this week" on Week and "Push to Jira" on Today. `RailPeriod` carries
      the label; `web/index.html:101` stops being the only place it is written
- [x] **R4** The rail is fixed beside a scrolling viewport, so the total, the split and Push never
      leave the screen — the scroll moves to the content region
- [x] **R5** The rail has a hover treatment on nav rows and a `:focus-visible` treatment throughout,
      consuming `--color-hover-rail`
- [x] **R6** In the instance split the name is rail-muted and only the figure is lifted to rail ink
- [x] **R7** Switching views resets the content region's scroll position
- [x] **R8** Both dividers are drawn, at the drawn thickness and radius: below the split and below
      Push, above the nav
- [x] **R12** Rail geometry matches the drawing: the rail's own gap and top padding, the split bar's
      height and its distance above the figures, and the spacing between figure rows
- [x] **R13** The rail's Settings mark is the prototype's `#p-gear`, transcribed — a toothed cog
      outline with a 3.3 centre at the drawn 1.7 stroke, not the hand-drawn circle-and-spokes the
      app ships. **Raised by the owner on 2026-09-09** while watching the running app. Two documents
      disagreed and that is why it survived: the prototype draws the cog, while decision 6 of the
      second-edition visual language lists the set as including "the three nav marks already drawn
      in `index.html`", which blesses the shipped figure. **That line is corrected too.** The
      placement stays where it is; [CB#89](89.md) moves Settings out of the nav and owns where the mark lives

##### The drawing, and a gate

      day-total push markers**, which two specs require and the footer omits
- [x] The first edition's "nav sits directly under Push with no divider" is corrected, since R8
      overrides it
- [x] The prototype's Today and Week headers are redrawn to Decision 12: both refreshes labelled,

- [x] **A lint fails when a declared design token has no consumer.** It runs in the existing lint

      shipped. It catches nothing that has no token, so it is a net rather than a proof
- [x] `make verify` and `make test-e2e` are both green, and their output is in the pull request
