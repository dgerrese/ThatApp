# CB#309: tooltip.spec's accessible-name test is flaky in Chromium: the hover tooltip sometimes never opens

Archived from Codeberg issue [#309](https://codeberg.org/DionGerrese/ThatApp/issues/309), closed. Opened by DionGerrese on 2026-10-09, closed on 2026-10-21.

Labels: `bug`.

Milestone: v1.

**Why it is kept:** Root-cause investigation of the Chromium tooltip flake (hover before the split chunk upgrades, no second pointerenter), the owner's decision to fix the test helper and split off the product half, and the :hover-drops-at-upgrade finding; plan 2026-10-14-mounttrigger-waits.md cites this body as design of record.

## Body

Found on 2026-10-09 in a full `make test-e2e` run on a local scratch merge of PRs CB#298, CB#304, CB#306 and CB#308. None of them changes `that-tooltip` or `tooltip.spec.ts`.

#### Problem

`tooltip.spec.ts:632` — "the trigger's accessible name is unchanged by the tooltip's presence" — failed once in Chromium under the full suite: after `trigger.hover()`, the fixture tooltip stayed hidden for the full 5s.

```
Locator:  locator('that-tooltip[data-fixture]')
Expected: visible
Received: hidden
  > 637 |   await expect(page.locator("that-tooltip[data-fixture]")).toBeVisible();
```

Alone it passed 20 of 20.

#### Cause

`that-tooltip` loads from its own split chunk (`main.ts`), and it adds its `pointerenter` listener only when it upgrades. The spec's `mountTrigger` helper returns before the upgrade, and seven hover tests hover straight after it. Under load the chunk arrives late, the hover lands first, and Chromium sends no second `pointerenter` while the pointer rests — so the tooltip never opens. The two hover tests that wait for `that-tooltip[data-fixture][popover]` first (lines 460 and 518) have no flake on record.

Reproduced on 2026-10-12 with a probe that delays the tooltip chunk by 1.5s: in Chromium, hover without the wait failed 3 of 3 with `Received: hidden`; with the wait it passed 3 of 3. (The delay did not take effect in Firefox, so the probe says nothing about Firefox.)

[#24](https://github.com/dgerrese/ThatApp/issues/24)'s tests at lines 801 and 834 use the same helper with no wait, and [#24](https://github.com/dgerrese/ThatApp/issues/24)'s comment of 2026-10-08 records the same "never opened" symptom on line 834. Not confirmed: [#24](https://github.com/dgerrese/ThatApp/issues/24)'s other symptom, `boundingBox()` returning null after the tooltip was visible.

#### Approach — owner's decision, 2026-10-12

Fix the test helper; the product does not change here. `mountTrigger` waits for the upgrade before it returns, as the two stable tests already do by hand. A product change — a tooltip that opens when it upgrades under a resting pointer — was considered and split out, because a probe showed Chromium drops `:hover` on the trigger after the upgrade for a reason not yet known. That is its own intake ticket.

#### Acceptance criteria

- [x] `mountTrigger` in `tooltip.spec.ts` waits for `that-tooltip[data-fixture][popover]` to be attached before it returns.
- [x] The by-hand waits it makes redundant are removed (the one in `mountTriggerAfterButton` and the one in the Escape-by-hover test at line 460).
- [x] A new test delays the tooltip chunk (routing the script whose body defines `that-tooltip`), calls `mountTrigger`, hovers, and expects the tooltip visible. With the helper's wait removed it fails in Chromium (mutation shown in the task report).
- [x] The full `tooltip.spec.ts` passes in both browsers, and `make test-e2e` passes.

#### Out of scope

- Any change to `that-tooltip.ts` — see the intake ticket for the product half.
- [CB#307](307.md), the Firefox tab-return flake. Different file, different cause.
- Closing [#24](https://github.com/dgerrese/ThatApp/issues/24). It is at intake and is the owner's; if this fix holds, it can close after the merge.

### DionGerrese, 2026-10-14 11:50

Two rulings made in the autopilot run of 2026-10-14. The owner may reverse either.

1. **The helper's wait names the mounted tooltip by `for`.** The ticket writes `that-tooltip[data-fixture][popover]`. "One Escape closes every open tooltip" mounts two fixtures, and a strict `toBeAttached` on two matches throws. So `mountTrigger` waits for `that-tooltip[data-fixture][for="<id>"][popover]`. The intent is the same.
2. **A third redundant wait also goes:** `that-tooltip[for='second'][popover]` in the "one Escape" test. It is redundant for the same reason as the two the ticket names.

### DionGerrese, 2026-10-14 12:01

A second cause, found at the gates of the autopilot run on 2026-10-14. **Ruling 3 — the owner may reverse it.**

The full `make test-e2e` with Task 1 in place failed once: `[chromium]` "Escape closes a tooltip opened by hover…", `Received: hidden`. The tooltip had upgraded and `reveal()` had run, so it opened and then closed.

Cause, confirmed by a probe: the fixture trigger is appended to `body` after Today's own content, at the window's bottom edge. `hover()` scrolls it into view. When Today's issues answer lands after the hover, the empty state renders and moves the trigger out from under the pointer, and `pointerleave` closes the tooltip. With `/api/issues` delayed 1500 ms the hover failed 6 of 6 in both browsers; answered at once, it passed 6 of 6.

I fix this on this branch, in the test: a shared `openToday` waits for Today's empty state before any test mounts, with a regression test that delays the issues answer. Reasons: it is this ticket's own symptom, the last criterion asks for a green `make test-e2e`, and the fix stays in the test as decided. The product does not change: a tooltip that closes when its trigger moves away from the pointer is correct.

### DionGerrese, 2026-10-14 12:09

Correction to ruling 3's cause, from the whole-branch re-review (verified in `web/src/router.ts`): the trigger is not moved by Today's render. From `md:` up the shell is `md:h-screen` and `main#content` scrolls inside itself, so the fixture trigger always sits at y=720, just below the fold. `hover()` scrolls the window to bring it into view. When a navigation's render resolves after that, the router's `resetContentScroll()` runs `window.scrollTo(0, 0)`, the trigger goes back to y=720, and `pointerleave` closes the tooltip. The fix stands: Today's empty state is written in the same task as that reset, so `openToday`'s wait is the right settle signal. The comments and the plan are corrected on the branch.
