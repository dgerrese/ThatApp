# CB#284: Hosted Forgejo Actions will be disabled for this repository

Archived from Codeberg issue [#284](https://codeberg.org/DionGerrese/ThatApp/issues/284), closed. Opened by Codeberg.org on 2026-10-06, closed on 2026-10-07.

**Why it is kept:** Codeberg's notice disabling hosted Actions, citing pipeline volume and its Terms of Use clause on mostly AI-written projects; this is the root reason for leaving Codeberg, while the skill records only 'run volume'.

## Body

This repository has been found to launch a high amount of pipelines on our hosted Forgejo Actions offering. At the same time, this is apparently due to a development workflow which makes heavy use of generative LLMs.

Please note that a recent change to our terms of use has [added restrictions](https://codeberg.org/Codeberg/org/src/branch/main/TermsOfUse.md#:~:text=You%20must%20not%20share%20projects%20that%20mostly%20consist%20of%20code%20written%20by%20%22generative%20AI%22%2Dtools%20%28including%20services%20such%20as%20Claude%2C%20OpenAI%20Codex%29%2E). Our [blog post](https://blog.codeberg.org/protecting-our-floss-commons-from-llms.html) has explained why [especially heavy CI/CD setups](https://blog.codeberg.org/protecting-our-floss-commons-from-llms.html#:~:text=The%20development%20team%20of%20none) are an issue for us.

Therefore, we'll disable hosted Forgejo Actions for this repository and kindly ask you to check your compliance to our Terms of Use.
