## Body

Closes CB#209

#### What changed

`startTimer` and `stopTimer` in `web/src/timer-state.ts` now run through one module-level guard, `oneAtATime`, which covers the whole action: the stop dialog's chunk load, the dialog, the POST and the state update. A call made while another is in flight returns at once and does nothing; the guard is released in a `finally`, so Keep running, Esc, a failed POST and a failed chunk load all leave the next click working.

The guard is module-level, so the rail's stop, a row's stop and another row's play all share it. So:

- a double click on stop while the dialog first loads opens **one** dialog, with one `#that-stop-dialog-heading`;
- a click on another row's play while a start's POST is out sends nothing.

Both callers (`that-timer.ts`, `rail.ts`) are unchanged: a dropped call resolves quietly, and neither caller ties a focus move, busy state or announcement to it. No button is disabled or restyled — out of scope, per the ticket.

#### Tests

`.claude/e2e/tests/timer-guard.spec.ts` is a new file, so it does not collide with the other branches that append to `timer.spec.ts`. It holds the stop dialog's chunk with `page.route("**/chunk-*.js")`, registered after `goto`; the rig already blocks the service worker, so the route sees the request. Six tests, both browsers: row double stop, rail double stop, row stop while the rail's dialog loads, a play while a start's POST is held, and the next stop after Keep running and after a failed stop. Before the guard, the first four failed in both browsers (8 of 12); the last two guard the release and passed throughout.

#### Review

Task review and whole-branch review both approved it. The whole-branch review's one fix — a count check that passed on its first sample — is in `09bda49`.

**A known ceiling, noted rather than fixed:** no timer POST has a time limit. If one stalls on the network (not fails), every play and stop in that tab does nothing until the browser gives up on the request. Before this change a second click sent another request. Fixing it means a time limit on the POSTs or a busy state, and the ticket rules the busy state out.
